# Privacy Policy for CountUp

**Last updated:** August 26, 2026

CountUp ("the App") is developed as a free, 100% offline utility. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to user data and privacy.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
CountUp does not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personal information, device data, or usage metrics. 

### 2. Permissions
The App requests zero Android permissions. It does not access your internet connection, device identifiers, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage.

### 3. Local Data Storage
All information you enter into the App (such as your count-up item names and anchor dates) is stored locally on your device only. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

### 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics
The App does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, tracking tools, or cloud analytics services.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because CountUp does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages.
