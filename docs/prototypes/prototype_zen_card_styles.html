Zen Minimalist Aesthetic Rules

• The Principle of Ku (空 / Emptiness): Cards are breathable vessels. Avoid saturated neon tones; draw instead from raw clay, mineral ores, steamed rice, and soot.

• The Ensō Circle (円相): The icon badge serves as the red seal (印章) or dew drop on an ink wash scroll, drawing the eye gently to the anchor.

• Rice Paper Dialogue: Every card surface must complement #F5E6D3. Soft celadon and ecru linen eliminate screen fatigue.
