🏛️ Android Design Expert Roundtable: The 1x1 Challenge
4 Perspectives
Android Launcher Specialist
"A 1x1 cell is ~70dp wide. Side-by-side icon + name + number is mathematically impossible without truncation. 1x1 MUST use vertical stacking or corner pips."
Zen Typography Architect
"A truncated 'SOBRIET...' destroys the mindful zen feel. Either wrap words across 2 lines or let the sacred number and glyph speak for themselves."
Glanceability UX Lead
"On a 1x1 app icon sized widget, users glance for the NUMBER and the COLOR/ICON. The title only needs to be identifiable, not a full paragraph."
Executive Recommendation
"2x1 remains the Gold Standard for full poetic clarity. If 1x1 is offered, **Variant A (Zen Monolith)** or **Variant B (Metric Stamp)** are the only shippable choices."
